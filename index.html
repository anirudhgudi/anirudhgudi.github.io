Mechatronics and Robotics Systems Engineer

I'm a mechatronics and robotics systems engineer passionate about robotics and automation. My expertise lies in designing and optimizing mechatronics and robotics systems through CAD, finite element analysis (FEA), and hands-on prototyping. I bridge the gap between mechatronics principles and robotic applications—transforming concepts into functional hardware.

Whether designing intricate mechatronics assemblies in CATIA or SolidWorks, conducting structural optimization using FEA, or prototyping robotic systems that solve real-world problems, I approach engineering with rigor and creativity. My toolkit spans CATIA, SolidWorks, ANSYS, MATLAB, and hands-on fabrication, enabling me to develop complete mechatronics and robotics solutions from concept through manufacturing and deployment.
