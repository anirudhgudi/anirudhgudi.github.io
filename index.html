Mechanical Engineer and Robotics Graduate

My name is Anirudh Gudi, and I am a mechanical engineer with a Masters degree in Robotics specializing in control and navigation. For as long as I can remember, I have had a burning passion for building robots and designing mechanical systems. I'm all about the engineering process going through several design iterations to optimize a product, rapid prototyping with CAD tools like CATIA and SolidWorks, and fine-tuning control systems is where I thrive the most. With 2+ years of automotive engineering experience and expertise in ROS 2, FEA, and sensor fusion, I bridge the gap between traditional mechanical design and cutting-edge robotics.

This website provides detailed write-ups for my engineering projects, showcasing the technical aspects of my work in robotics, control systems, and mechanical design.
